# Recap Quality Metrics

## Product metrics
- Citation coverage: percentage of decisions and follow-ups with source references.
- Freshness: age of the newest source included in a recap.
- Cross-functional reach: number of distinct roles that open each recap.
- Follow-up clarity: percentage of actions with owner and due date.

## Beta targets
- 90 percent citation coverage for decisions and follow-ups.
- 70 percent of beta recaps opened by at least two functions.
- 40 percent reduction in status-chasing messages.

## Reporting notes
Low-confidence recaps should count separately from published recaps so launch reporting does not overstate trusted output.
